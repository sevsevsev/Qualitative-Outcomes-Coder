## Cycle-01b: independent verification of the 19 unverified sources

**How it ran:**

- Two verifier agents worked in parallel on 32 claims (each one a code-to-source
  link).
- They saw only the claim, the citation, the claimed component, and the code's
  definition. They did not see any proposal reasoning.
- They were limited to WebSearch/WebFetch and could not rely on memory.

**Results:**

| Verdict | Count |
|---|---|
| SUPPORTED | 23 |
| PARTIAL | 8 |
| UNSUPPORTED | 1 |
| MISATTRIBUTED | 0 |
