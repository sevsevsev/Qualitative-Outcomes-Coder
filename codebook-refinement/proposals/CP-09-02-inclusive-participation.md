## Problem

A2.4 excludes "Youth participating alongside peers (their participation)" and Y2.6 includes
"participating fully alongside peers". Severin coded both such gold rows A2.4:

- S001 "Youth with disabilities will participate fully in program activities alongside their
  peers." Gold A2.4; app Y2.6 (high) in both 3.0 runs (design-set miss).
- H058 "Youth with disabilities participated in inclusive recreational activities alongside
  peers without disabilities." Gold A2.4.

Also S098 "Parents will access interpretation and translation services to communicate with
teachers." Gold A2.4 (A2.4 already includes interpreters); app F1.7 in both runs, reading
"communicate with teachers" as navigation.

## Change

**A2.4** include, before: `Sensory kits and visual schedules; accessible materials; interpreters; home-language communications.`
after: `Sensory kits and visual schedules; accessible materials; interpreters; home-language communications; youth with disabilities (or another group facing barriers) taking part alongside peers, when inclusion is the point.`
Exclude, before: `Youth participating alongside peers (their participation); families navigating systems.`
after: `A young person's choice to join or return; families learning to navigate systems.`

**Y2.6** include: remove `participating fully alongside peers` (see CP-09-01 for the full
after-text). Use instead, after: `A2.5 program-level counts and attendance; A2.4 inclusion of youth with disabilities alongside peers.`

**rulesText rule 10**, append:

```
Families using interpretation or translation to communicate with the program or school -> A2.4. Families learning how a school or service system works -> F1.7.
```

## Why this is the smallest fit (S2.2)

The text and two consistent adjudications disagree; changing the include and exclude lines is
the smallest change that removes the contradiction. The alternative, keeping the text and
having Severin move S001 and H058 to Y2.6 (rule 1, who changes), is listed in the review and
not recommended.
