## Primary source (current)

What Works Clearinghouse, *Study Review Protocol*, "Version 5.0 (March 2023)".
https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/WWC/Docs/ReferenceResources/WWC-SRP50-508.pdf

SRP 5.0 replaced the topic-specific protocols (secondary, postsecondary, dropout and others) with one list of domains. It introduces the list with: "The list of outcome domains below defines the domains that are eligible for review by the WWC".

## Older source (superseded, backup)

*WWC Evidence Review Protocol for Dropout Prevention Interventions, Version 3.01 (March 2014)*: https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/WWC/Docs/ReferenceResources/wwc_dp_protocol_v3.0.pdf
- "Progressing in school": "Includes outcomes that assess the number of high school course credits the student has earned"
- "Completing school": "Includes outcomes that measure whether the student has earned a high school diploma or GED"
- "Staying in school": "Includes outcomes that measure whether the student has dropped out of school"

## Caveats

- SRP 5.0 has no domain named "General Academic Achievement" or "General Achievement". Older secondary and postsecondary protocols used those labels, but I did not fetch them. The current equivalent is "Academic Achievement" (Pre-K—12 / Postsecondary). It covers only **broad standardized assessments across multiple subjects**. That supports Y1.15's "achievement not tied to one subject" but does not cover unmeasured or self-reported "doing better in school". Record this as a **partial** fit.
- Grades and GPA are not under "Progressing". SRP 5.0 has separate "Course Performance" domains. I did not quote them; a verifier should check whether they fit Y1.15's grades component better.
- The SRP 5.0 page offers no separate "credit accumulation" domain. Credits fall under Progressing in Pre-K—12 Education or Progressing in College.
- The protocol index page (ies.ed.gov/ncee/WWC/Document/257) lists only Transition to College protocols. A v4.0 link there returned "File Not Found".
