## What the researchers missed

1. **Child-welfare entry is one DHS truancy-provider template.** It accounts for 12 NLM rows across 8 "Truancy Provider" programs, coded Y3.4 (9), F1.1 (2) and F2.3 (1). Those rows are 9 of Y3.4's 10 NLM rows. Eight program rollups therefore show a family-relationship outcome they don't have. Make one review-desk correction once R-26 has an owner, as with the Y1.8 template (decision E).
2. **The same text gets different codes.** "Increased engagement in science" went to Y2.2, and "Increase engagement in science" went to Y2.3. "Increased engagement" went to Y2 domain-only, while "Increased student engagement" went to Y1.9. About 5 in-session engagement texts sit in Y2.6. This is the evidence item 7 needs.
3. **Adult and general-public outcomes are coded in Part Y.** Examples: "More Philadelphians value the arts" (Y2.4), "More Philadelphians participate in the arts" and "families will become audiences" (Y2.6), "Empowered communities with increased self-expression" (Y2.5). That is about 6 texts. Decision C does not apply, because the codes' text is about young people; the coding is what goes wrong. These texts belong in A3.3 or uncoded, not in a Y code. "Bigs and Littles experience joy/connection/safety" can stay in Y codes, since the youth is a subject. This is the adult-volunteering lead in this batch.
4. **Multi-outcome statements mostly work.** Secondary codes catch the second outcome ("public speaking and teamwork" Y4.6+Y4.7; "self-awareness and emotional regulation" Y4.1+Y4.2). The one loss I found is "focus and stress management", where Y4.2 is primary and focus is dropped. Splitting is not a Y2-Y4 priority.
5. **Digital and career skills barely touch Y2-Y4.** Bare "soft skills" goes to Y4 domain-only consistently, which is right and matches batch2's "life skills" hint (R-31). One misroute: "clarity in academic and career decision-making" went to Y4.10. It belongs in Y7.1 or Y7.2.
6. **Arts appreciation still sits in Y2.4** ("appreciation design in their environment", "Make connections between art and life"). CP-09-04 in PR #62 will move such texts to Y2.5. Re-check the Y2.4 counts after #62 merges.
7. **Gold coverage.** No adjudicated gold row exists for bare self-awareness, focus, bare leadership, bare resilience, subject engagement, generic behavior or generic risk. S3.1 needs two gold rows per side, so each hint above should ship with proposed rows (`status=proposed`) for Severin.
