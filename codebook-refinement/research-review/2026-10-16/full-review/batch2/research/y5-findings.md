## 2. Code by code (Q2, Q3, Q6)

### Y5.1 Self-Understanding & Identity Exploration: keep

Nagaoka integrated identity is direct, and CASEL "Integrating personal and social identities" is
partial. The include line "articulating one's values" goes slightly past both. CASEL 2020
Self-Awareness also lists "Linking feelings, values, and thoughts" (fetched, verbatim bullet,
https://casel.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/sites/5/CASEL-Framework-and-Skills.pdf). The registry's

component (claim y5-12). Real use: 18/17 texts ("Increased self-awareness", "reflect on identity"),
coded as intended. Confidence medium: the gap is small.

### Y5.2 Cultural & Social Identity: fix citation

- **Racial identity (CP-10-04).** CP-10-04 justifies "racial" through Nagaoka, whose fit is *partial*
  (the verifier says the list of social identities is only an example inside "consistency across
  identities"). Muhammad's Pursuit 1 is **direct** and names race: "such as culture, race, gender, and
  more" (registry excerpt). LFJ's 2022 standards do not name race in the Identity standards. When
  CP-10-04 is finalized, cite Muhammad as its support. Confidence high.
- **Sexual orientation, gender identity and linguistic identity are named by no cited source.** LFJ
  2022 does not contain "sexual orientation", "LGBT", "gender identity" or "ethnicity" (checked on the
  PDF). Nagaoka and Muhammad name gender, not sexual orientation. Adjudicated S129 (self-acceptance of
  sexual orientation and gender identity) depends on this half. This is an unlogged departure; it
  would be a codebook-defined element under S1.6. Candidate fix: NASEM (2019) *The Promise of
  Adolescence*, ch. 2 (https://www.nationalacademies.org/read/25388/chapter/5): "Adolescence is also
  a time when identities or sense of self related to gender and sexuality are developed and
  solidified" and "making sense of one's ERI is a normative developmental process that often begins in
  adolescence". This is tier B and cross-sector, but it describes development, not pride or
  acceptance, so expect a partial fit (claim y5-09). For "linguistic": CASEL Self-Awareness
  "Identifying personal, cultural, and linguistic assets" (fetched; claim y5-10; partial, since it is
  about assets, not identity).
- **Pride.** LFJ ID.9-12.4, "I express pride and confidence in my identity without perceiving or
  treating anyone else as inferior.", fits "pride" directly.
- **Real use:** 6/0 texts ("experience Black pride", "pride in their cultural heritage"), coded as
  intended.

Smallest change: fix citation (add NASEM, CASEL bullet; re-date LFJ). If NASEM verifies only as
partial, log an FD (codebook-defined element "sexual identity", counted) rather than dropping it, so
S129 keeps its code. Confidence medium.

  act with agency in one role or setting" (claim y5-08). It is an example, so expect partial.
- **Athlete, reader, writer** remain unnamed. If the verifiers rate y5-08 partial, log FD sector

### Y5.5 Purpose & Future Orientation: re-anchor the faith element (FD-P12)

FD-P12 (and FD-005, which it carries) says: revisit "if a verified framework for youth spiritual
development is found". One exists, inside a cross-sector PYD framework that also covers the rest of
the code. Catalano, Berglund, Ryan, Lonczak & Hawkins, *Positive Youth Development in the United
States* (SDRG, University of Washington; published by HHS ASPE,
https://aspe.hhs.gov/reports/positive-youth-development-united-states-research-findings-evaluations-positive-youth-development-0),
lists 15 PYD constructs, including "Fosters spirituality", "Fosters belief in the future", "Fosters
self-determination", "Fosters self-efficacy" and "Fosters clear and positive identity".

- Spirituality: "Programs were classified as fostering spirituality if they promoted the development
  of beliefs in a higher power," followed by "internal reflection or meditation, or supported youth in
  exploring a spiritual belief system," and "or sense of spiritual identity, meaning, or practice."
  (The fetch tool returned this sentence in pieces; the verifier should record one ≤40-word
  contiguous excerpt.) Claim y5-01.
- Belief in the future: "Belief in the future is the internalization of hope and optimism about
  possible outcomes." Claim y5-02.
- Caveats for the verifier. The constructs are defined as what programs foster, so the fit to a
  youth outcome needs judging. The ASPE page prints no publication date (the grant date "September,
  1996" appears), so the year is unconfirmed. Attribution: the SDRG authors, not ASPE, which
  published it (S1.4). Tier C.
- Purpose itself: CASEL 2020 Self-Awareness "Developing interests and a sense of purpose" (fetched,
  tier B; claim y5-03; partial, since it pairs interests with purpose). This also lifts Y5.5 above
  its tier-D-only support. Damon, Menon & Bronk (2003) define purpose as "a stable and generalized
  intention to accomplish something that is at once meaningful to the self" / "and of consequence to
  the world beyond the self" (https://coa.stanford.edu/sites/default/files/devofpurpose_0.pdf). That
  is narrower than the code (the "beyond the self" part), so it is optional (claim y5-15).
- Alternative for the faith element: Search Institute's spiritual development definition, with three
  processes: "Awareness or awakening", "Interconnecting and belonging", "Living an integrated life"
  (https://www.search-institute.org/our-research/youth-development-research/spiritual-development/;
  tier D; same publisher as the current Y5.5 source; claim y5-14).
- Nagaoka (2015) does say "Values serve as broad guidelines for living and provide an orientation for
  one's desired future." (full report). This is a Values factor, not purpose. Do not cite it for Y5.5;
  it confirms the earlier "unsupported" verdict.

Smallest change: add Catalano (spirituality, belief in the future) and the CASEL bullet to Y5.5's
source line, and add Catalano to Y5's `frameworkBasis`. Set `fidelity: framework` and close FD-P12.
Y5.5 then joins two Catalano constructs (belief in the future and spirituality), so record it as a
merged-components entry (logged, not counted) rather than deleting the history. The Y6.1 boundary
("faith growth or spiritual purpose" → Y5.5) and gold S016, S124 (Y5.5) and S125, S145 (Y6.1) are
unaffected. FD-005 belongs to the retired 2.x codebook; note there that its revisit condition is met.
Enum +0. Confidence medium-high (it depends on the verifier accepting program-construct wording).

Real use: 19/14 texts ("ignite a sense of purpose", "more positive view of their future",
"educational aspirations"). Faith texts: 0 in both runs. Educational and college aspirations land in
Y5.5 consistently, since only career aspirations are excluded. Leave that boundary until PR #62
(Y7.1/Y7.2) merges.

### Y5.6 Agency, Self-Determination & Self-Advocacy: fix citation

The code names "advocating for one's own needs", "self-advocacy in IEP meetings" and "requesting
accommodations". The registry says Nagaoka agency is "verified" but notes "Self-advocacy is not named
explicitly", and Search personal power is partial ("does not cover ... self-advocacy"). The
self-advocacy half therefore has no source: an unlogged departure. Adjudicated S032 and S236 depend
on it.

- Catalano et al., self-determination: "Self-determination is the ability to think for oneself, and
  to take action consistent with that thought." Programs qualified if they built capacity "for
  empowerment, autonomy, independent thinking, or self-advocacy". This is cross-sector and direct for
  both halves (claim y5-04).
- 29 U.S.C. 733(b)(5), pre-employment transition services: "instruction in self-advocacy, which may
  include peer mentoring." (https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/29/733). Tier A. Its population
  is "students with disabilities", which matches the code's IEP examples (claim y5-05). It is a
  required service, not an outcome, so expect partial.
- Shogren et al. (2015) Causal Agency Theory (in `basis`, partial, "[VERIFY: journal and pages]" in
  research-basis.md): drop it if Catalano verifies.

Real use: 25/17 texts ("engage in self-advocacy", "Girls advocate for themselves", "provide a sense of
agency"), coded as intended; CP-09-08 rule 7 already settles the voice boundary. Confidence high.

## 3. Taxonomy (Q4)

The three categories match Nagaoka's outcomes and components reasonably: identity → integrated
identity; self-beliefs → mindsets; agency → agency. Purpose has no Nagaoka component, which is why
Y5.5 is the outlier. Catalano's PYD constructs map onto Y5 almost one to one (clear and positive
identity, self-efficacy, belief in the future, spirituality, self-determination). They are a stronger
single structural fit than Nagaoka for categories B and C. I do not recommend re-anchoring the domain
now: Nagaoka's update is due, and a second anchor switch in a month would be churn. Add Catalano to
`frameworkBasis` as supporting, and decide after the Consortium release. A well-established
construct is missing a home: resilience (Catalano's "Fosters resilience"; see Y5.4). Nothing should
be merged or split; Y5.4's earlier merger of self-worth into confidence still has no gold pressure.
