## What the researchers missed

1. **Interviewing is ambiguous.** Journalism programs use "interviewing" for reporting (Y7.2,
   above).
2. **"Sexual" in Y5.2 pulls in sexual health.** Girls Inc.'s "Manages reproductive health — Positive
   feeling about own sexuality" was coded Y5.2. Rewording the definition from "sexual" to "sexual
   orientation" (S129 unaffected) would send it to Y8.4. That is 1 row; it is a lead for the
   Y5.2 CP.
3. **Lists and compounds are left unsplit.** "Career-readiness skills (communication, financial
   empowerment, self-advocacy, and teamwork)" went whole to Y7.3. "Workplace skills and
   entrepreneurial mindsets" went to Y7.1. Many "X and resilience" texts were also left whole. The
   prompt already says to split lists. This is an enforcement and eval issue (split-multi-outcome
   lead), and it is bigger than any tie-breaker here.
4. **Bare "digital literacy" goes to Y1.4.** Three real texts (PYN, Penn Talent Search, Mindset of
   Champions) code Y1.4 under the 2.x CF-024 logic. Whatever PR #62 decides, its judge should test
   these texts. The "work or daily life" wording does not decide them, because they carry no
   qualifier.
5. **Volume.** Y6.7 (5 texts), Y5.2 (6), Y6.3 (11): citation work there is for fidelity, not
   accuracy. Prioritize the resilience and life-skills rules.
6. **Adult volunteering is one program.** AmeriCorps Seniors' senior volunteers are the program's
   participants, so batch 3/4 should decide whether "volunteers" means workforce (A1) or
   beneficiaries (F) by who the program serves, not by the word.
