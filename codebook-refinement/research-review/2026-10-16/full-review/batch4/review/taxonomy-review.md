# Batch 4 (A1, A2, A3): taxonomy and methods review (2026-10-16)

Reviewer: taxonomy/methods. Held-out gold not opened. Convention (as in batches 1-2): a PARTIAL source can be
cited with `fit: partial`, but it does not close a gap where the code's text goes past it. Decision C (an FD
only when a code's own text names a population outside its source) is applied throughout.

## 1. Overall judgement

Part A's axis (who changes: workers, the program, or something beyond it) is applied consistently. The
category level is again the weak one:

- **A1**: the categories follow **Guskey (2002) Levels 2 and 4** plus a workforce block (a1 §5 is right). But
  `frameworkBasis` names Learning Forward and the Surgeon General, and research-basis.md says the split
  "follows Learning Forward's distinction". Learning Forward is verified only at `fit: partial` (design
  standards, not outcome levels). So the structure's real anchor is not named as the anchor.
- **A2**: there is no anchor. Four unrelated sources (YPQA, UW Outputs, Mapp & Kuttner, connectivity
  statutes), and categories A/B/C are codebook groupings. The Urban COF (a2-11) covers only A2.2 and A2.5.
- **A3**: Reisman et al. (2007) already sits in `frameworkBasis` and its outcome categories yield A3.1
  (Improved policies) and, once a3-01 lands, A3.2 (Strengthened alliances). That is the closest thing in
  batch 4 to a real anchor-derived category set. A3.3 (Sampson) sits outside it, and category B pairs
  organizations (A3.2) with residents (A3.3), which is a grouping, not a dimension of one construct.

Recommendation (PATCH, docs only): research-basis.md should say that the A2 categories and A3 category B are
codebook-defined groupings, that A1's learning/practice split comes from Guskey, and that A3.1 now rests on
CP-10-02, not Learning Forward (a3 §1).

## 3. Methods notes

- **Domain-only is being used as a residual bin.** CP-09-09 made A3/none mean "names the domain but no
  specific result". a3's A3.3 option (a) and the school-climate hint send *specific* results ("community-wide
  healthy behaviours", "improved school climate") to A3/none. The derived `specificity` column will then call
  them "domain", which misdescribes them. Severin should decide whether domain-only may also mean "specific,
  but no code" (or flag `codebook_gap`) before both PATCHes.
- **Gold coverage is thin in Part A.** A1.3 has one adjudicated row (S149), A3.3 one (S154), A2.6 none, and
  the A3.1 rows (S019, S101, H089, plus H028 as an alternate) are all policy. a3 missed H089. None of the
  proposed boundaries can be tested on design gold yet. Propose `status=proposed` rows before any CF.
