# Panel determinations on the open edge cases (E-01 to E-57)

Codebook 3.1.5 (`youth_outcomes_v3`). Panel of four, working blind to each other: a qualitative methodologist (M), a youth-development practitioner (Y), a measurement and frameworks specialist (F) and a skeptic (S). The chair combined their files (`expert-*-edges.md` in this folder). Three or four agreeing settles a point; on a 2-2 split or a three-way spread the chair decided, preferring the smallest change (STANDARDS S2.2) and the reading that follows what changes and for whom. Where a vote is shown as "3-1", the dissenter is named.

These are recommendations. Nothing here marks a gold row adjudicated or edits STANDARDS.md; items that need Severin's own hand are marked **[Severin]** and gathered in `panel-actions.md`. No new citation is relied on.

"Gap flag" means `codebook_gap` = 1 (decision G9).
