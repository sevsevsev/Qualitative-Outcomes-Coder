# Expert panel on codebook 3.1.5: report for Severin

## Who sat and how it worked

Four reviewers worked separately and blind to each other: a qualitative methodologist, a youth-development practitioner, a measurement and frameworks specialist, and a skeptic whose job was to argue for the smallest change. Each coded all 75 proposed example statements and ruled on 57 open questions. Three blind coders had already coded the statements with today's prompt. Three or four agreeing settled a point; otherwise I decided as chair, preferring the smallest change and the reading that follows who changes. Blind-coder agreement counted as evidence that a definition is clear, not that the production model will follow a hint. Nothing was marked as gold.

## The examples

All 75 are settled. All four reviewers agreed on the code for 71, three of four on one, and I decided three. Of the 69 examples with a single intended code, 67 kept it. The six left open by their writers were settled. All three blind coders reached the panel's code on 59.

Notable changes:

- A teen launching a business and making sales goes to Employment & Earnings (Y7.5), not Technical Skills, Credentials & Work-Based Learning (Y7.4).
- "More certified district teachers" goes to Staff & Volunteer Knowledge & Skills (A1.1). A count of people earning a credential is the same result as one person earning it.
- A parent enrolling a child in Medicaid goes to Income, Benefits & Financial Stability (F2.1), because the parent is the subject.
- "Entrepreneurial mindset" goes to Agency, Self-Determination & Self-Advocacy (Y5.6), as initiative already does.
- Bare "resilience" defaults to Emotion Regulation & Coping (Y4.2).

Wording fixes from examples the blind coders got wrong:

- Parents' stress about bills went to Caregiver Well-Being & Resilience (F1.4); add an exclude pointing to F2.1.
- A school adopting a curriculum went to Practice Change (A1.2); limit its include to program staff.
- Tutors staying in teaching after service went to Workforce Stability & Well-Being (A1.3); name that case in its exclude.
- School culture went to Program Quality (A2.1) and to Policy & Institutional Change (A3.1); it needs the school-climate hint.
