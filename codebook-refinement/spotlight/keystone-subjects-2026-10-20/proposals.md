# Proposals from the Keystone subjects search (2026-10-20)

Drafted by the thread from the four searches (`candidates_K1` to `K4`) and the checks (`verification/results_A` to `C`,
claim ids KV-001 to KV-039; `verification/claim_index.csv` maps claim to candidate). Link ids refer to the 173-row map
on pull request 72 (`_already_in_map.md`). Lane 1 = a test of a Keystone subject; lane 2 = high school academics broadly.

## P-01 Strengthen L-03 (Mathematics, Y1.3 -> Keystone Algebra I, S10), lane 1
Add the first evidence on an Algebra I end-of-course exam: Saga's live-online tutoring in New York City high schools,
39 vs 31 percent passing the Algebra I Regents by the end of ninth grade (matched comparison; KV-002), and the hybrid
randomized trial, 0.23 standard deviations on math tests (KV-003, treatment on the treated). Tier stays B (good).
Conditions unchanged (daily, in-school, small groups).

## P-02 Extend the mechanism link L-83 (Attendance and School Stability, Y1.13 -> tests) to S10 and S12, lane 1
Within-student comparison across classes: missing 10 classes lowers math or English test scores by 3 to 4 percent of a
standard deviation and course grades by 17 to 18 percent (secondary school; KV-034). Chicago's on-track study: course
passing is primarily determined by attendance (KV-035). Proposed: L-83 gains S10 and S12 as a mechanism link, at its
current tier only if one study is enough; otherwise a new research-associated link Y1.13 -> S10, S12.

## P-03 New tested link, lane 2: Academic Mindsets and Self-Efficacy (Y1.12) -> ninth-grade grades (step to S4)
National randomized trial of a short online growth mindset lesson: lower-achieving ninth graders' core grade point
average up 0.10 grade points (KV-031); independent MDRC analysis confirms grade point average, math grade point
average and fewer failing grades (KV-032). L-167 (no reliable test-score gain) stays. Tier: suggestive, lane 2 only,
condition: the specific online lesson delivered in school, lower achievers.

## P-04 New tested link, lane 2: family utility-value mailings (Supporting Learning at Home F1.2; Relevance and Utility
Value Y2.4) -> high school math and science course-taking and ACT math and science (step toward S10, S11)
One randomized trial in Wisconsin: nearly one more semester of math and science in grades 11 and 12 (KV-013); ACT
math and science up 12 percentile points (KV-014). One team, one sample. Tier: suggestive, lane 2.

## P-05 Do-not-claim row: student relevance (utility-value) writing (Y2.4) -> high school science grades
One randomized trial, gains only for students with low expectations (KV-012). Below the bar alone. Not recommended.

## P-06 Do-not-claim rows: college-access programs and high school grades or tests, lane 2
- Upward Bound national trial: more math credits only, no other academic preparation effect (KV-017).
- Upward Bound Math-Science: higher math and science grades, more chemistry and physics, one matched study (KV-015, KV-018). Below the bar.
- College Possible: no effect on ACT scores in a 238-student trial (KV-020).
- iMentor College Ready: few impacts on achievement (KV-021).
- Text-message college nudges: no SAT effect nationally (KV-033).
- GEAR UP Iowa: one matched study (KV-019), below the bar.

## P-07 Advanced coursework (Y1.16) -> Advanced Placement exams, lane 2, college and career goal (no SPOTlight indicator)
National Math and Science Initiative College Readiness Program: positive in two matched designs (KV-022, KV-023);
Texas predecessor positive in difference-in-differences studies (KV-024, KV-025); but the only randomized trial found no
first-year gain and a lower passing rate (KV-016), and the newer computer science version was null (KV-026). College
Board personalized outreach raised exam taking 1.1 points (KV-027). Proposed: record, not recommended, because the
randomized trial disagrees.

## P-08 Learning Strategies and Study Skills (Y1.11) -> ACT and SAT, lane 2, college and career goal
Clearinghouse review: test preparation and coaching programs have positive effects on general high school achievement,
improvement index +9 (KV-030). Proposed: tested, suggestive, college and career goal only; no SPOTlight indicator.

## P-09 Gap notes: Keystone Literature (S12) and Biology (S11) have no partner-runnable tested model
- Literature: the effective high school reading programs are school-run courses (Enhanced Reading Opportunities,
  0.11 on state English tests in ninth grade, faded next year, KV-005; Vaughn two-year intervention 0.43 comprehension,
  KV-006; Chicago Structured Literacy 0.20, preliminary, KV-007). Reviews: none of 12 effective adolescent programs was
  in a high school (KV-009); no United States secondary tutoring study qualified (KV-008); 4 of 10 Striving Readers
  programs positive (KV-039).
- Biology: early college high schools raised Biology end-of-course passing 68 vs 53 percent (school model; KV-010);
  secondary science reviews favour classroom instructional process programs (KV-011); none is a partner model.
- Algebra I school models for context: double-dose algebra (KV-001), Cognitive Tutor year 2 in high schools (KV-004),
  small effects for most curricula (KV-038).
Proposed: one not-recommended row per subject, "school model, not a partner link", so the map shows the gap honestly.

## P-10 Research-associated, lane 2: self-control (Self-Regulation, Y4.2) -> later report-card grades
Two longitudinal samples adjusting for earlier grades or IQ: self-control predicted changes in report-card grades
better than IQ, not test scores (KV-037). Grades are not an indicator. Proposed: not recommended for SPOTlight;
note only.

## P-11 Research-associated: Academic Mindsets and Self-Efficacy (Y1.12) -> later achievement, extend L-91 to high school
Meta-analysis: self-beliefs predict later achievement with a path of 0.08 after prior achievement (KV-036; ages not
confirmed). Proposed: extend L-91's indicators to S10 and S12 only if the age range can be confirmed; otherwise no change.

## Not taken forward
Becoming a Man grade point average (KV-028): the replication at new sites found no significant effect on student
outcomes (L-16), so a first-trial grade result adds nothing claimable. Sponsor-a-Scholar (KV-029): unreachable.
